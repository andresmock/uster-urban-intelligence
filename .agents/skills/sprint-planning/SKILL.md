# Sprint Planning

Follow `AGENTS.md` and the development governance documents. State that `$sprint-planning` is active.

## Preconditions

- The human has identified product priorities or requested planning.
- The team is in PLAN mode and has not been authorized to implement.
- GitHub access is verified before relying on Issue, Project, or Milestone state.

## Roles

Alex coordinates and Sam owns requirements analysis. Involve Ada, Bob, Eve, or Max only for relevant architecture, delivery, or quality input.

## Procedure

1. Review confirmed priorities and relevant GitHub backlog items when accessible.
2. Identify a small demonstrable outcome; ask Sam to clarify value, requirements, and measurable acceptance criteria.
3. Obtain specialist input needed to expose dependencies, risks, unknowns, and validation needs.
4. Propose a focused Sprint Goal, candidate Issues or tasks, Definition of Done, and execution order.
5. Separate confirmed requirements from assumptions and proposed backlog changes.
6. Present the sprint proposal and wait for explicit human approval.

Do not create or modify Issues, assume backlog items are approved, or assign story points without an agreed method.

## Output

Report the Sprint Goal, user or business value, candidate Issues or tasks, acceptance criteria, dependencies, risks, Definition of Done, proposed order, assumptions, and required human decisions.

## Approval and stopping

Sprint scope and goal require human approval. Stop after the proposal; do not enter EXECUTE, alter GitHub state, or begin another sprint.

## Failure and escalation

If priorities, acceptance criteria, GitHub state, capacity, or dependencies are materially unknown, report the gap and propose the smallest discovery action. Never invent requirements, Issue state, estimates, or capacity.
